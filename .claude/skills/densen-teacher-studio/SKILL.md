# DENSEN Teacher Studio Engineer

## Goal

Provide verified dance teachers with professional tools to create, publish, manage, and analyze dance education content.

## Teacher Features

Teacher Studio may include:

- Teacher profile
- Verification status
- Moves
- Combos
- Choreographies
- Classes
- Courses
- Lessons
- Challenges
- Students
- Analytics
- Pricing
- Revenue information

## Teacher Verification

Teacher accounts may have statuses such as:

- PENDING
- VERIFIED
- REJECTED

Do not provide verified-teacher capabilities to unverified accounts.

## Workflow

1. Check teacher authentication.
2. Check teacher verification status.
3. Verify ownership of teacher-created content.
4. Verify publishing permissions.
5. Verify student access.
6. Verify pricing permissions.
7. Verify content visibility.
8. Respect copyright and music-rights requirements.
9. Preserve moderation controls.
10. Protect teacher analytics and financial information.

## Content Publishing

Support appropriate states such as:

- DRAFT
- REVIEW
- PUBLISHED
- UNPUBLISHED
- REJECTED

Do not automatically publish content when review is required.

## Students

Teachers should only access student information that the product explicitly allows them to access.

Do not expose unnecessary personal information.

## Analytics

Analytics should provide useful information without exposing private information belonging to unrelated users.

## Final Check

Before completing Teacher Studio work:

- Verify teacher permissions.
- Verify verification status.
- Verify content ownership.
- Verify publishing permissions.
- Verify student access.
- Verify pricing.
- Verify privacy.
- Verify moderation.
